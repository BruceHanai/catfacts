Information we collect

When you interact with the Cat Facts SMS service, we may collect your mobile phone number, messages you send to the service, subscription and opt-out status, and technical information necessary to operate the service.

How we use information

Information is used only to operate the SMS service, process opt-in and opt-out requests, deliver Cat Facts to subscribers, screen inbound messages, maintain service security, and comply with applicable requirements.

Cat Facts subscribers receive approximately one message per day. Message and data rates may apply.

Mobile information and consent

Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, and messaging consent information are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information may be processed by service providers solely as necessary to operate the messaging service, deliver messages, maintain infrastructure, prevent abuse, or comply with law.

Opting out

You may opt out of Cat Facts at any time by replying STOP. You may reply HELP for assistance.

Data retention

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain subscription preferences, prevent unwanted messaging, resolve technical issues, and comply with applicable legal or carrier requirements.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the operator of the Cat Facts service through the phone number associated with the service.
