Cat Facts SMS

Cat Facts is a recurring SMS program operated by Bruce Hanai for people who explicitly choose to subscribe.

How to subscribe

Text CATFACTS to (619) 815-2244.

By texting CATFACTS, you consent to receive recurring automated, non-promotional Cat Facts from Bruce Hanai.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.

After you opt in, you will receive this confirmation:

Cat Facts from Bruce Hanai: You're now subscribed to receive recurring Cat Facts. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply MEOW or HELP for help, STOP to cancel.

Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. Reply HELP or MEOW for help.

Terms & Conditions · Privacy Policy

About this number

This phone number may also receive ordinary inbound personal communications. Sending an ordinary message does not enroll the sender in Cat Facts. Only the CATFACTS keyword enrolls a sender in the recurring Cat Facts program.
